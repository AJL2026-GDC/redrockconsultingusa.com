What we do

Three lines of work, one team.

We work for buyers and sellers who need a product found, checked and delivered, and for owners and investors who need a large real estate project thought through before money moves.

01

Commodity consulting and trading

Hard and soft commodities, from metals and energy to grains, proteins and freeze-dried products. We advise on a trade or carry it out.

02

Import, export and sourcing

We go to the source. Products are sourced directly from producers and moved to the buyer, with the paperwork and logistics handled along the way.

03

Real estate consulting

Consulting for large real estate transactions and development projects, in connection with Red Rock Holdings and Red Rock Capital.

How we work

From a request to a delivered product.

The same four steps apply whether you are buying or selling.

  1. Tell us what you needThe product, specification, quantity, destination and timing.
  2. We go to the sourceWe look for the product with producers and suppliers and check what is offered.
  3. Agree the termsPrice, delivery, documents and payment are set out in a contract before anything moves.
  4. DeliverWe follow the shipment through to delivery and stay with the file until it closes.
